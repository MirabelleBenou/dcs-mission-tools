**DCS Mission Tools 2.2.0** — the Squadron Ready Room family insignia, and one version number.

### Highlights
- **The family insignia**: the home screen shows the Mission Tools insignia above the suite line — served by the Ready Room, your wing's emblem instead. The browser tab carries the family's icon, each tool shows it before its short name in the top bar, and a *Squadron Ready Room family* line sits above the credits of the home screen and of each tool's empty screen.
- **One version number**: it no longer shows in the top bar or in the suite line, only once, at the bottom of the home screen.

Full list in the [CHANGELOG](https://github.com/MirabelleBenou/dcs-mission-tools/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
