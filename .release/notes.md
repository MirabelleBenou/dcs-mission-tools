**DCS Mission Tools 2.1.0** — a layout made for the phone, and the aircraft's equipment in the Loadout Editor.

### Highlights
- **On a phone** (under 600 px wide, or a folding phone closed): every tool gets a one-line header, a one-row bottom bar where *⋯* holds *Load another .miz*, import and export and *Undo all*, and filters folded behind a *Filters (n)* button. The Ground Unit Swapper and the Loadout Editor show their lists as cards; in the Ground Unit Swapper, tapping a unit opens a panel to change its type and skill. A tablet, an unfolded phone or a computer keeps the layout as it was.
- **Loadout Editor — aircraft equipment**: *Settings* now set the DCS mission editor options that weigh (IR exhaust suppressors, extra armour, cargo door, MW-50 tank…) for the checked aircraft; only what you change is written.
- **Loadout Editor — weight like the DCS mission editor**: gun rounds and equipment are counted; empty and maximum weights come from the DCS mission editor for every aircraft.
- **Same words everywhere**: questions back out with *Keep*; search hints say what they search.

Full list in the [CHANGELOG](https://github.com/MirabelleBenou/dcs-mission-tools/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
